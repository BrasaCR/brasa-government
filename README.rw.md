# BRASA Government

** Amakuru ya leta yateguwe hafi yinzego na serivisi abaturage bakoresha. **

[Fungura BRASA Government](https://brasagovernment.net/) · [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Uburezi](https://brasa.education/) · [Ubucuruzi](https://brasa.business/)

BRASA Government iha buri gihugu urubuga ruhoraho rw’imirimo ifitiye igihugu akamaro mu rwego rwo kubungabunga imiterere nyayo y’igihugu. Urupapuro ruhuza abenegihugu na minisiteri zemewe, amashami, ibikorwa rusange, ibigo bya leta, hamwe nubutunzi bwo gukurikirana.

## Ibyo buri gihugu cyigihugu kirimo

- Nyobozi, amategeko, ubucamanza, nizindi nzego zubahiriza itegeko nshinga
- Minisiteri, amashami, ibigo, n'inzego za Leta
- Amashanyarazi, amazi, lisansi, itumanaho, interineti, imyanda, hamwe n’abatanga isuku
- Uburezi, ubwikorezi, ubuzima, umurimo, ubucuruzi, indangamuntu, nizindi serivisi zabaturage
- Inkomoko yemewe n'amatariki yo kugenzura

## Igihugu neza

Ibihugu ntabwo byose bifite imiterere imwe ya leta. BRASA ikoresha uburyo busangiwe bwo kureba, ariko ibigo numubare wimpapuro bigenwa namakuru yigihugu yagenzuwe aho gukopororwa mubindi bihugu.

## Kubona ururimi

Imiterere yicyongereza ikomeza guhindurwa. Amazina ya minisiteri ninzego arashobora kugumana imiterere yururimi rwibanze, mugihe impapuro rusange zaho zakira URL zitandukanye kandi zigasubiramo inyandiko.

## Shaka guverinoma yawe

Tangira kuri [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) kugira ngo ugere ku rubuga rwa leta rw’igihugu cyawe.

## Tanga umusanzu

Menyesha ihuriro ryashaje, utange isoko ya leta, cyangwa ufashe gusuzuma amakuru yimirimo ifitiye igihugu akamaro.

## BRASA uyu munsi

BRASA ubu ihuza aho ibihugu 198 bigana n'Uburezi, Guverinoma n'Ubucuruzi. BRASA Business ifite ibice 12 bihujwe: kugurisha n'inyemezabwishyu, CRM, ibarura, abakozi n'amahugurwa, imishahara na banki, igihe, ubufasha bw'umuryango, amategeko, impapuro za leta, raporo n'ahantu ho gukorera hamwe. Harimo kandi igitabo cy'ibaruramari gifunguye, inzira z'akazi ziyobowe no gukomeza gukora nta murandasi.

BRASA Financials ikataloge sisitemu 385 zo kwishyura zivuye mu nyandiko 395, zirimo ibisobanuro by'amasoko 161 n'ubwoko 216 bwo kwishyura: amafaranga ya telefoni, kohereza ako kanya, QR, amakofi y'ikoranabuhanga, imiyoboro y'amafaranga, kohereza amafaranga, open banking, amakarita yo mu gihugu na USSD.

Kuba muri kataloge ntibisobanura ko byatangiye gukora. Abatanga serivisi bafite uruhushya ni bo bimura amafaranga; buri buryo bugomba kugenzurwa ku gihugu n'inkomoko, uruhushya, ihuzwa, igerageza ry'ubwishyu n'icyemezo cyo gukoresha.
