# BRASA Government

**Iinkcukacha zikaRhulumente eziququzelelwe kumaziko kunye neenkonzo ezisetyenziswa ngabahlali.**

[Vula i-BRASA Government](https://brasagovernment.net/) · [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Imfundo](https://brasa.education/) · [Ishishini](https://brasa.business/)

URhulumente we-BRASA unika ilizwe ngalinye iwebhusayithi engaguqukiyo yenkonzo yoluntu ngelixa egcina ubume bokwenyani belizwe. Amaphepha anxibelelanisa abemi kumasebe asemthethweni, amasebe, amaziko oncedo, amaziko karhulumente, kunye nemithombo yokuhlola.

## Yintoni equlethwe yisayithi yelizwe ngalinye

- Amaziko alawulayo, owiso-mthetho, ezomthetho, kunye namanye amaziko omgaqo-siseko
- Abaphathiswa, amasebe, ii-arhente, kunye namaqumrhu karhulumente
- Umbane, amanzi, amafutha, unxibelelwano, i-intanethi, inkunkuma, kunye nogutyulo
- Imfundo, ezothutho, impilo, abasebenzi, ishishini, isazisi, kunye nezinye iinkonzo zabemi
- Imithombo esemthethweni kunye nemihla yokuqinisekisa

## Ukuchaneka kwelizwe

Amazwe akanabume obufanayo borhulumente. I-BRASA isebenzisa isakhelo esibonwayo ekwabelwana ngaso, kodwa amaziko kunye nokubalwa kwamaphepha kumiselwa ngedatha yelizwe eqinisekisiweyo kunokuba ikopishwe kwesinye isizwe.

## Ukufikelela kulwimi

Ulwakhiwo lwesiNgesi luhlala luhleli. Amagama oMphathiswa kunye neziko anokugcina iifomu zawo ezisemthethweni zolwimi lwengingqi, ngelixa amaphepha oluntu asekuhlaleni afumana ii-URL ezahlukeneyo kunye neerekhodi zokuphonononga.

## Fumana urhulumente wakho

Qala nge-[BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) ukuze ufikelele kwisiza sikarhulumente selizwe lakho.

## Yiba negalelo

Xela ikhonkco elisemthethweni eliphelelwe lixesha, unike umthombo karhulumente, okanye uncede uphonononge ulwazi lwelizwe lenkonzo yoluntu.

## BRASA namhlanje

BRASA ngoku idibanisa iindawo zamazwe ali-198 neMfundo, uRhulumente kunye noShishino. BRASA Business ineendawo ezili-12 ezidityanisiweyo: intengiso neerisithi, CRM, isitokhwe, abantu noqeqesho, umvuzo neebhanki, ixesha, inkxaso yosapho, umsebenzi wezomthetho, iifomu zikarhulumente, iingxelo nendawo yokusebenza ekwabelwana ngayo. Ikwabandakanya ileja evulekileyo, iinkqubo ezikhokelwayo kunye nokuqhubeka ngaphandle kwe-intanethi.

BRASA Financials idwelisa iinkqubo zentlawulo ezingama-385 ezivela kwiirekhodi ezingama-395, kuquka iinkcazo zemarike ezili-161 kunye neentlobo zentlawulo ezingama-216: imali yeselula, ukudluliselwa kwangoko, QR, izipaji zedijithali, uthungelwano lwemali, ukuthumela imali, open banking, amakhadi asekhaya kunye ne-USSD.

Ukudweliswa akuthethi ukuba sele kuvuliwe. Ababoneleli abanelayisensi bahambisa imali; indlela nganye ifuna ukuphononongwa kwelizwe nomthombo, ilayisensi, ukudityaniswa, uvavanyo lokuhlawula kunye nemvume yokuvelisa.
